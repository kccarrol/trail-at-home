      One-time setup

      Uploading in one tap needs a free Strava API app and a small helper that keeps its secret safe. The steps are in the README under "Sending rides to Strava". Without them, you can still download rides as TCX files.

      Getting a GPX file from Strava
      1. Open strava.com in a browser (the export option isn't in the Strava app).
      2. For a saved route, open the route and choose Export GPX. For a ride, open the activity, tap the ⋯ menu, and choose Export GPX.
      3. Tap Add GPX routes and choose one or more files. Routes are saved on this phone only.

      Settings

      Hill difficulty sets how much of each hill you feel. At 100% climbs and descents are true to the route; at 50% they're half as steep, in both your speed and the suggested resistance; at 0% everything rides flat.

      Example: with 25 on flat ground and 4 levels per 1%, a 6% climb suggests 49. On descents resistance drops by half as much. Adjust until climbs feel right for your legs.

      When the bike shares power, your speed is worked out from your watts, your weight (plus 13 kg of bike), and the trail's grade, as if riding a mountain bike on hardpacked dirt. Climbs slow you down, descents carry momentum, and speed tops out at 56 mph. If you stop pedaling for 5 seconds, the ride clock pauses until you start again.
