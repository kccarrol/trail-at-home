        Info

        Getting started

        1. Add routes in Browse routes, from GPX files (see below).
        2. Tap Start a ride, choose a route, and connect the bike if you like.
        3. Pedal. The screen shows how steep the trail is where you are and which resistance level to set on the bike.

        Getting a GPX file from Strava

        1. Open strava.com in a browser (the export option isn't in the Strava app).
        2. For a saved route, open the route and choose Export GPX. For a ride, open the activity, tap the ⋯ menu, and choose Export GPX.
        3. In Browse routes, tap Add GPX routes and choose one or more files. Routes are saved on this phone only.

        What the trail signs mean

        The elevation profile on the ride screen uses its own colours for steepness: blue downhill, green 0–3%, yellow 3–6%, orange 6–10%, red 10% and up.

        Connecting your VeloCore

        On the bike's screen, go to Programs and start the third-party app workout, then tap Connect bike and pick the bike. The app reads power, cadence and speed, and heart rate if you connect a monitor. If the bike disconnects mid-ride, the ride carries on as if you'd stopped pedaling while the app reconnects.

        How speed and resistance work

        When the bike shares power, your speed is worked out from your watts, your weight (plus 13 kg of bike) and the trail's grade, as if riding a mountain bike on hardpacked dirt. Climbs slow you down, descents carry momentum, and speed tops out at 56 mph.

        The suggested resistance is your flat-ground level plus a number of levels for each 1% of grade (half as much on descents). Hill difficulty in Settings scales both the speed effect and the suggestion.

        While you ride

        • If you stop pedaling for 5 seconds, the ride clock pauses until you start again.
        • The upper profile shows the trail just ahead; the strip below shows the whole route. Tap the strip to jump to a spot.
        • A note appears 1 km before each climb. Tap it to hide it for that climb.
        • End ride saves the ride to your history. Your spot on the route is kept, so you can continue it another day.

        Sending rides to Strava

        Every ride can be downloaded as a TCX file from its summary or from Ride history, then added at strava.com/upload/select in a browser (Strava's phone app can't import files).

        For one-tap uploads there's a one-time setup, about 15 minutes and free, best done on a computer. Strava only gives upload access to apps that keep a "client secret" private, which a web app can't, so the secret lives in a tiny helper you run on Cloudflare.

        1. Create a Strava API app at strava.com/settings/api. Name it "Trail at Home", category "Training", and set Authorization Callback Domain to just this app's host, for example yourname.github.io. Note the Client ID and Client Secret.
        2. Create the helper. At dash.cloudflare.com (free plan), create a Worker from the "Hello World" starter, choose Edit code, paste in strava-helper-worker.js from the app's files, and deploy.
        3. Give the helper its settings under Variables and Secrets: STRAVA_CLIENT_ID, STRAVA_CLIENT_SECRET (as the encrypted "Secret" type) and ALLOWED_ORIGIN, this app's origin such as https://yourname.github.io. Deploy, and copy the Worker's address ending in .workers.dev.
        4. Connect the app. In Settings, under Strava, open One-time setup, enter the Client ID and helper address, tap Connect Strava, and leave "Upload your activities" ticked.

        Uploads are marked as virtual rides and never added twice. Rides that can't upload right away (no signal, Strava busy) go the next time the app opens online.

        Your data

        Routes, rides and settings are stored only on this phone. Nothing leaves it unless you upload a ride to Strava.

        Settings

        Resistance

        Hill difficulty sets how much of each hill you feel: 100% is true to the route, 50% makes hills half as steep, 0% rides flat. Example: with 25 on flat ground and 4 levels per 1%, a 6% climb suggests 49.

        Display

        Strava

        Not set up.

        With positions, the ride appears on the real route's map on Strava. It's marked as a virtual ride, but people may still take it for an outdoor ride. Untick this to send only distance, elevation, speed, power, cadence and heart rate.

        One-time setup

        Uploading in one tap needs a free Strava API app and a small helper that keeps its secret safe. Without them, you can still download rides as TCX files.
